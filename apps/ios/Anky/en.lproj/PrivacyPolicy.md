# Anky Privacy Policy

Effective and revised: October 16, 2026

Anky is a writing app that keeps your writing on your phone. Starting with version 2.2, the Anky app does not connect to any Anky server. There is no account, no login, no analytics and no advertising.

## 1. Who we are

Anky is operated by **Anky, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation. Contact us at **[support@anky.app](mailto:support@anky.app)**.

This policy covers the Anky iOS app and the anky.app website. Anky is not a medical, mental-health, crisis, legal or financial service.

## 2. What stays on your device

Anky stores these things on your iPhone only:

- Your writings, any unfinished draft, and the index used for the list of past writings and your stats
- Your settings: duration, the 8 second rule, backspace, autocorrect, font and size
- If you use the Screen Time gate: the apps you chose (as opaque Apple tokens), the gate's state and your unlocks
- A random key, kept in the iOS Keychain, used only to encrypt your optional iCloud backup

None of this is sent to Anky. We cannot read your writing, and we have no copy of it.

## 3. What Anky does not do

- It does not send your writing anywhere.
- It does not use AI. If you choose **Copy prompt + writing**, the text goes to your clipboard, and from there only where you paste it. Whatever service you paste it into handles it under its own terms.
- It does not include analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
- It does not sell, share or rent anything, because it collects nothing.

## 4. Apple services you can choose to use

- **iCloud backup:** if you turn it on, Anky encrypts your writings with AES-GCM and stores the encrypted file in your own iCloud Drive. The key can be saved to your iCloud Keychain so you can restore on a new phone. Apple runs iCloud; Anky never sees the backup.
- **Screen Time:** if you turn on the gate, Apple's Screen Time frameworks handle permission and app selection. Your list of apps stays on the device.
- **Notifications:** reminders and the gate's notifications are scheduled locally by iOS.
- **Face ID:** used only to confirm it is you before turning on iCloud backup. Apple handles the check; Anky only learns yes or no.

## 5. Support

Settings can open your mail app or a chat link. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and then we receive what you chose to write.

## 6. Earlier versions

Versions of Anky before 2.2 could send a writing to Anky's server when you explicitly asked for an AI reflection, and used RevenueCat for subscriptions. Those versions did not store your writing on the server. To delete any records tied to an earlier version, write to **[support@anky.app](mailto:support@anky.app)**.

## 7. Deleting your data

Delete a single writing from its menu. **Settings → Delete All Data** removes every writing, setting and key from the device and deletes the Anky iCloud backup. Deleting the app also removes local data.

## 8. Children, changes and contact

Anky is not intended for children under 13. We will revise the date above if this policy changes.
