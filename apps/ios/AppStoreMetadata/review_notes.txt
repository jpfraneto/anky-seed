Anky 2.3.0 (73) — notes for review

WHAT CHANGED IN THIS VERSION
- A first-launch welcome: three short lines on what Anky is for, then the user sets up the page (day or night, font, size, whether backspace is allowed) and may opt in to one daily local reminder notification.
- Anky Pro, an auto-renewable monthly subscription (anky.monthly) with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers, is now required to use the writing page. Purchases use StoreKit 2 directly; there is no server and no account.
- A writing is always 8 minutes; it ends after 8 seconds without typing.
- Settings: ten fonts, eight ink colors, a day/night toggle.
The app still makes no network connections of its own. App Privacy: Data Not Collected.

HOW TO TEST (no account or login required)
1. On first launch, tap "continue" through three lines, tap one option on each setup step, then choose "remind me at …" (asks for notification permission) or "not now".
2. The paywall shows the price, the trial, Restore, Terms and Privacy, and the auto-renewal terms. Subscribe with a Sandbox Apple ID.
3. Type on the page. The clock counts down 8:00. Stop typing for 8 seconds and the writing ends; it stays on the page.
4. On a finished writing the "…" list (top right) is open: Copy prompt + writing, Copy writing, Delete. Tap the page to keep writing.
5. The menu button (top left) opens the drawer: past writings, Settings and Stats.
If the user has writings and no subscription, the paywall always offers "take your writings with you" (a plain text export), so nobody loses their writing.

NO CRYPTOCURRENCY FUNCTIONALITY
Anky has no wallet, no blockchain integration and no tokens. A random key is generated on the device and stored in the Keychain; its only use is to encrypt the optional iCloud backup of the user's writings. It is never shown to the user and never sent anywhere.

SCREEN TIME (FamilyControls)
Blocking is opt-in: Settings → Blocked apps, turn on "Block until I write", grant Screen Time access and choose apps. Please also allow notifications when asked. iOS does not let a shield open another app, so the shield's buttons post a local notification; tapping it opens Anky.
- "Write" opens Anky on a blank page. Finishing a sentence unlocks the chosen apps for 15 minutes (up to three times a day); writing the full minutes unlocks them for the rest of the day. Without a subscription, "Write" unlocks the apps for the rest of the day.
- "Emergency unlock" unlocks the apps for the rest of the day immediately. No writing and no payment are required.
The same switch in Settings → Blocked apps turns blocking off at any time. Only Anky's own shield is applied or removed.
