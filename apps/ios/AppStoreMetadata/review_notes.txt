Anky 2.2.0 (67) — notes for review

WHAT CHANGED IN THIS VERSION
Anky is now a fully offline writing app. This version removes features; it adds no new capabilities or entitlements.
- No server connection of any kind. AI reflections, the subscription (Anky Pro, RevenueCat) and all network code were removed. The app has no in-app purchases.
- One writing page with a side drawer of past writings. A finished writing stays on the page; tapping it continues it.
- Tapping the clock shows the 8 second rule, a minutes slider (1–8) and ∞ (no time limit).
- The App Privacy answers are now "Data Not Collected".

NO CRYPTOCURRENCY FUNCTIONALITY
Anky has no wallet, no blockchain integration, no tokens and no payments. A random key is generated on the device and stored in the Keychain; its only use is to encrypt the optional iCloud backup of the user's writings. It is never shown to the user and never sent anywhere.

HOW TO TEST (no account or login required)
1. Launch the app and type on the page. By default a session ends after 8 seconds without typing.
2. The writing stays on the page. Tap it to keep writing.
3. On a finished writing, the "…" button (top right) offers Copy writing, Copy prompt + writing (puts the text on the clipboard with a reflection prompt) and Delete.
4. Tap the menu button (top left) to open the drawer: past writings, Settings and Stats.

SCREEN TIME (FamilyControls)
Blocking is opt-in. Go to Settings → Blocked apps, turn on "Block until I write", grant Screen Time access and choose one or two apps. Please also allow notifications when asked.

iOS does not let a shield open another app, so the shield's buttons post a local notification; tapping it opens Anky.
- "Write" opens Anky on a blank page. Finishing a sentence unlocks the chosen apps for 15 minutes (up to three times a day); writing the chosen number of minutes unlocks them for the rest of the day.
- "Emergency unlock" opens Anky and unlocks the apps for the rest of the day immediately. No writing and no payment are required.

Nobody can be locked out of their device: the same switch in Settings → Blocked apps turns blocking off at any time. Only Anky's own shield is ever applied or removed. The app does not change any other Screen Time setting.
