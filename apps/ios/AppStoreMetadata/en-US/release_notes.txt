The simplest Anky yet.

Anky no longer connects to any server. No AI reflections, no subscription, no account. Your writing never leaves your phone.

Tap the clock for one line: the 8 second rule, a slider for your minutes, and ∞.

Finished a writing? Tap it to keep going. Same page, same words.

Backspace is on by default. Turn it off in Settings if you want to write forward only.

Copy any writing, or copy it with a reflection prompt to paste into the AI you already use.

Stats are simpler: you wrote that day or you didn't.

Blocked apps now unlock as soon as you have written, and the shield's emergency unlock works again.
