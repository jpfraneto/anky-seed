Write for eight minutes. Don't stop.

Anky is a page and a clock. You write for eight minutes, and if you stop typing for eight seconds, the writing ends. No going back over the last sentence, no polishing. Just what is there.

HOW IT WORKS

The first time you open Anky, you set up your page with a few taps: day or night, your font, its size, and whether you can erase. If you want, Anky can send you one quiet reminder a day.

After that it is always the same: open the app and you are already writing.

WHEN YOU FINISH

Your words stay where they are. Copy them with one tap, or copy them inside a reflection prompt to paste into whatever AI you already use, if you want to. Tap the page to keep writing.

Every writing is kept in a drawer that slides in from the side, dated and searchable. Stats shows your streak and the days you wrote.

MAKE IT YOURS

Ten fonts. Eight inks. Four sizes. Day or night. Backspace and autocorrect, on or off.

YOURS, AND ONLY YOURS

Anky has no server and no account. What you write never leaves your iPhone: it is never uploaded, never read and never used to train anything. No analytics, no ads. If you want a backup, turn on encrypted iCloud backup and it goes to your own iCloud.

WRITE BEFORE YOU SCROLL

If you want, Anky can stand between you and the apps you reach for without deciding to. Choose them in Settings. When you open one, write first.

ANKY PRO

Anky is a monthly subscription. New subscribers start with 3 days free; the price is shown in the app before you subscribe. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel any time in your App Store account settings. Your writings are always yours: you can take them with you whether or not you subscribe.
