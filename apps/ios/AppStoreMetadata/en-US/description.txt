Go for a walk. Or stay right where you are.

Anky is a quiet place to express what comes while you move through your day: through your neighborhood, along a beach, in a forest, or from a chair by the window. Nothing to achieve, nothing to count. Just you, paying attention.

HOW A SESSION WORKS

Begin a session and choose how to express yourself:

• Write. The page moves only forward. Stop typing for eight seconds and the writing rests, or turn that off and decide for yourself. Choose a length, or no limit at all.
• Talk. Speak out loud and Anky records your voice. Lock the screen, put the phone in your pocket and keep walking; it keeps recording until you stop.
• Record. Film what is in front of you, or yourself.
• Or simply be silent.

Move between them within the same session. When you are done, the session becomes a memory you can return to: read the writing, listen to the voice, watch the video.

YOURS, ON YOUR IPHONE

Every writing, voice recording and video is saved on your iPhone. Recordings are never uploaded and never transcribed. There is no feed, no followers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Anky does not use your location.

Export everything, whenever you like: one file with every session, the original audio and video, your writings and your reflections. Delete it all from Settings at any time.

SEVEN DAYS FREE

Your first seven days with Anky are free, starting with your first session. No payment details are needed, and nothing is charged when the seven days end.

After that, starting new sessions needs a subscription. Everything you have already expressed stays readable and exportable, with or without one. Your memories are never held back.

THE SUBSCRIPTION

Anky is sustained by its subscribers, not by advertising. One auto-renewing monthly subscription keeps Anky open for new sessions and adds Reflect with Anky.

The price is shown in the app, from the App Store, in your currency. Payment is charged to your Apple Account only when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically each month unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account's subscription settings. Restore purchases from Settings.

REFLECT WITH ANKY (OPTIONAL)

Subscribers can ask Anky to reflect on a single writing. It happens only when you tap it, and Anky tells you first what will be sent: the text of that one writing goes to Anky's server, an AI model writes a reflection, and the server does not keep your writing. The reflection is saved on your iPhone. Voice and video are never sent. Nothing else in Anky needs AI.

WRITE BEFORE YOU SCROLL

If you want, Anky can stand between you and the apps you open without deciding to. Choose them in Settings; when you reach for one, write first. Turn it off any time.
