A blank page, and nothing standing in front of it.

No feed. No followers. No setup. You open Anky and you are already writing.

HOW IT WORKS

Eight minutes is the default. So is the rule that makes it work: if you stop for eight seconds, the session ends. And there is no backspace. You cannot go back and fix the last sentence, so you go forward and write the next one.

That is the whole trick. Writer's block is usually just editing too early. Anky takes editing off the table until you are done.

Every default is yours. Change the length, the stillness, the backspace, the typeface.

WHEN YOU FINISH

Your writing is sealed into a private archive on your phone, dated and searchable. Nothing is published. There is no audience, and that is the point.

From there you can ask Anky for a reflection: it reads what you wrote and tells you what it noticed. The thing under the thing. The question you circled for eight minutes without answering.

Or you can record yourself reading your own words aloud, and keep the video.

WRITE BEFORE YOU SCROLL

Anky can stand between you and the apps you open without deciding to. Write your minutes first, and the rest of the day opens.

WHAT IT COSTS

Writing is free. It will always be free. The blank page, the timer, the archive, the recording — none of that is behind anything.

Reflections are the part that costs money to run, so that is the part you pay for. Buy them in packs, or subscribe to Anky Pro for reflections from the strongest model available. If you would rather not pay at all, long-press the copy button and take your writing to whichever AI tool you already use. Anky will help you do that.
