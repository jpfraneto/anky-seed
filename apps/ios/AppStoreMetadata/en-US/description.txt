A blank page. Nothing in front of it.

Anky is the simplest writing app we could make. You open it and you are already writing. No account. No feed. No followers. No AI. Nothing to set up and nothing to unlock.

HOW IT WORKS

Tap the clock to choose how long you want to write, from one to eight minutes, or ∞ for no limit.

The hourglass next to it is the 8 second rule: stop typing for eight seconds and the writing ends. It keeps you moving forward instead of going back over the last sentence. Turn it off and only you decide when you are done.

That is the whole app.

WHEN YOU FINISH

Your words stay exactly where they are. Tap the page and keep going, today or any other day.

Every writing is saved on your phone, dated and searchable, in a drawer that slides in from the side. Copy a writing out with one tap, or copy it wrapped in a reflection prompt to paste into whatever AI you already use, if you want to.

Your streak, and a calendar of the days you wrote, are one tap away. You wrote today or you didn't.

YOURS, AND ONLY YOURS

Anky does not connect to any server. Your writing is never uploaded, never read and never used to train anything. There are no analytics and no ads. If you want a backup, turn on encrypted iCloud backup and it goes to your own iCloud.

WRITE BEFORE YOU SCROLL

If you want, Anky can stand between you and the apps you open without deciding to. Choose them in Settings. When you reach for one, write first. A finished sentence opens a short pass, and writing your minutes opens the rest of the day. Turn it off any time.

Settings has the rest: backspace, autocorrect, how long the stillness lasts, the font and its size.
